LECTURE: How to correctly use SOIC LTI Tool & Stage Analysis Tool    REF: CSLRC    (transcript: 20262 chars, 184 lines, covered: yes)

## 1. CRUX

The lecture teaches a trader how to correctly configure and read two SOIC-proprietary TradingView overlays -- the SOIC Stage Indicator (a shorter-horizon, cyclical-exit tool) and the SOIC Long-Term Indicator (a longer-horizon averaging-up tool) -- so the viewer stops guessing at the settings and can mechanically recognize a real entry/averaging signal versus noise (CSLRC 00:00:05-00:00:22).

## 2. MECHANISM

- Both tools combine the same two core primitives -- relative strength versus a benchmark (nifty, or whatever comparative symbol is chosen) and a volatility-stop (Vstop) line -- and read them together, never singly: "buy gets triggered when both volatility stop turn green, volatility stop turns positive and secondly the relative strength turns positive versus nifty" (CSLRC 00:04:46).
- Selling on the Stage Indicator is the mirror condition: "whenever we stop turns negative along with, along with relative strength of the stock falling, then it gives a sell signal" (CSLRC 00:05:27).
- The Stage Indicator (weekly chart) is explicitly positioned as an exit tool for cyclical businesses where the investor has no fundamental way to know when the cycle turns: "if you're looking at businesses which are cyclical and you need to have an exit strategy in place, then SOIC stage indicator on a weekly charts will help you a lot" (CSLRC 00:05:36), illustrated with RK Forge and Ador Welding.
- The Long-Term Indicator (LTI) adds two more primitives on top of relative strength + Vstop: RSI (14-period, buy signal at 45) and ADX (buy signal at 20, explicitly optional). The instructor lists all four buying conditions in sequence: "First is RSI crossing 45" (CSLRC 00:11:37), "Second is ADX crossing 20" (CSLRC 00:11:41), "Third is we stop turning positive" (CSLRC 00:11:44), and "Fourth is relative strength in higher than nifty" (CSLRC 00:11:48).
- Parabolic SAR is not an independent signal -- it is a derived flag that only turns positive once all four LTI conditions are simultaneously met, and its purpose is specifically to pace averaging up in tranches, not to trigger a first buy: "Parabolic SAR will only turn positive once all these four conditions are met and Parabolic SIR is for averaging up" (CSLRC 00:12:00).
- ADX is explicitly downweighted by the instructor's own stated practice: he personally sets ADX to 0 because it lags. "ADX cases sometimes buy signals late" (CSLRC 00:12:19) and "RSI, relative strength and VSTOP and Parabolic SR these four things are more than enough" (CSLRC 00:13:29), with the closing summary reaffirming "personally adx zero karke chalron" [likely "I personally run with ADX at zero"] (CSLRC 00:16:37).
- A technical signal is never sufficient alone -- it must be paired with a valuation/fundamentals check before acting: "you should be a little more validating on the basis of L3" (CSLRC 00:12:07), followed shortly after by "Don't just blindly keep chasing it" (CSLRC 00:12:17).

## 4. WHAT THE LADDER MISSES

(a) The ladder's `entry_rsi-001` rule (gate G8, weekly RSI >= 50, sourced from a different lecture, HOWB 00:01:55-00:02:23) conflicts with this lecture's stated RSI threshold for the same tool. This lecture states the buy signal is 45, not 50, in three separate places: "in RSI, the bi-signal is always 45, right?" (CSLRC 00:09:53), "First is RSI crossing 45" (CSLRC 00:11:37), and "RSI is above 45 right?" (CSLRC 00:15:14). This is the same indicator family (SOIC LTI) referenced in both lectures, so the ladder is currently gating CANDIDATE status on a threshold (RSI>=50) that this lecture's own source material for the same tool states as 45 -- either the ladder's threshold needs reconciling against this source, or the two lectures need to be understood as describing different thresholds for different contexts (the tool's own default buy-signal setting vs. a separate screener heuristic given elsewhere), a distinction the ladder does not currently make.

(b) The ladder's `entry_adx-001` rule (gate G8, weekly ADX >= 20) is treated as a hard gate contributing to a CANDIDATE/WATCH verdict. This lecture explicitly qualifies ADX as optional and describes the instructor's own practice as running with ADX at zero because it lags real signals: "ADX cases sometimes buy signals late" (CSLRC 00:12:19), "you can use ADX 20 or you can choose to ignore it as well" (CSLRC 00:12:24), and the closing summary "personally adx zero karke chalron" [likely "I personally run with ADX at zero"] (CSLRC 00:16:37), plus "So this is why I am using it as 0 right now" (CSLRC 00:17:11). He also states ADX's only role, when used at all, is as a lagging confirmation: he describes using "ADX you can use as 20 just for confirmation" (CSLRC 00:14:14). The ladder's gate treats this optional, deprioritized confirmation signal as load-bearing rather than as a confirmation-only add-on.

(c) The ladder has NO rule at all for the two signals this lecture calls central to both tools: Vstop state (positive/negative) and relative strength versus benchmark (positive/negative) -- see the SIGNALS section above for the exact quotes. These are two of the four stated buy conditions and both of the two stated sell conditions, and the instructor states the buy trigger requires all conditions jointly, not RSI/ADX alone. Since neither Vstop nor relative-strength-vs-benchmark is a screener.in ratio, this is a structural gap: the ladder's G8 gate (RSI + ADX only) captures roughly half of what this lecture defines as the actual entry signal.

(d) The ladder has no rule at all corresponding to the Stage Indicator's specific use case as a cyclical-business exit tool (RK Forge, Ador Welding examples, CSLRC 00:05:47-00:06:03) -- distinct from the existing exit-trigger machinery in the 38-name table, whose provenance is a different lecture (HOWB). This lecture gives no numeric threshold to add here, only a qualitative use case (weekly Stage Indicator recommended specifically for cyclicals whose cycle-end date is unknowable from fundamentals). Flagging as a gap the ladder has no rule for, not proposing a new numeric rule, since none is given in this lecture.

Nothing else in this lecture qualifies or contradicts a ladder rule beyond RSI/ADX above -- the settings guidance (comparative-symbol length 52/12, Vstop length 10, multiplier 2-2.5, parabolic SAR increment/max) is UI configuration, not a screening threshold, and is out of scope for the ladder.
