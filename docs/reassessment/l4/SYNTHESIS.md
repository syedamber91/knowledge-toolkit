# L4 Synthesis — When to Hold, Buy & Sell Using Technicals (19 lectures)

Sources: 19 reader briefs in `out/reassess_l4/briefs/` (all quote-gated ≥80%). Citations below name the brief (REF) that carries the transcript-verified quote.

## 1. The through-line

L4's collective argument is not "use technicals." It is: **fundamentals decide WHAT to buy; technicals exist to decide WHEN — and above all, when to SELL.** The course's own trailer states its reason for existing as "when to sell is something that no one else teaches us" (WWLTW), and the conclusion restates the division of labour verbatim (CWLCC). Technicals are never a buy reason: "I practice technicals only for screening to be honest" (DSEFD); "We will not buy anything like a technical analyst" (TVPDT); a technical signal must be validated against fundamentals before acting (CSLRC).

Within that frame, the course converges on one machine: a **trend-state system** — Volatility Stop (VStop, an ATR trailing stop), the 30-week EMA's direction, and relative strength vs a benchmark, confirmed by volume — where entries are multi-signal confluences and the exit is singular: VStop flips negative. Every parameter is scoped: monthly candles for core compounders, weekly for satellite/cyclicals; wider multipliers for high-conviction compounders, tighter for cyclicals; and the whole apparatus is explicitly regime-dependent and satellite-portfolio-weighted ("in my core holdings, I am not following this" — FSSMF).

The uncomfortable punchline for the ladder: **the two things G8 encodes (RSI, ADX) are the course's two most optional signals, and the things the course treats as load-bearing (VStop, 30-week EMA, relative strength, volume, and the entire exit side) are absent.**

## 2. What the ladder misses, ranked

1. **The exit side of G8 — VStop — is the course's centerpiece and the ladder has nothing.** ~10 of 19 lectures teach VStop as the sole sanctioned exit: "when is the exit getting triggered... when your Vstop becomes negative" (WBPNW, Dixon exit at ~3,800); "Exit has to be done on Vstop" (TVPD2); HGBYH devotes the whole lecture to it; MAEIM, TVPDT, ESRLE, RSCAR, CSLRC, SBFTS, FSSMF concur. Parameters: ATR × multiplier, **monthly candles for core/compounders, weekly for cyclicals/satellite** (HGBYH, TVPDT, ESRLE) — confirming Level 3's monthly-candle ATR finding as L4 doctrine. **HARD** to compute from OHLC the ladder already implies fetching; but see §3 on the multiplier — it must never be one constant.
2. **G8's entry gate encodes the wrong legs.** Where the course states entry conditions, VStop positivity is present every time and is called "the bare minimum requirement along with ADX above 20" (WBPNW); TVPD2 makes entry three legs (VStop + RSI>50 + ADX>20); ESRLE/CSLRC make it four (VStop + RS + RSI45 + optional ADX); MAAIM says ADX alone "won't give you any right conclusiveness" without VStop. The ladder kept RSI+ADX and dropped the leg every lecture requires. **HARD** (same data family).
3. **30-week EMA direction — now a three-course convergence.** WESNW makes it the primary entry trigger ("if the 30 weekly m is start saving upwards on back of higher volumes... the trend has changed"), weekly-only, with the 10w/30w golden crossover as the sharper variant; MAEIM makes its down-turn "the start of stage four... a selling pointer"; MSRTM builds stage 2 on it; FSSMF's composite sell is "30 weekly MN negative, RS negative, and V-stop negative"; TVPD2 uses it as trend context. With L5's two independent sightings, this is the best-evidenced missing rule in the project: **HARD**, both an entry condition (turning up on volume) and an exit condition (turning down / price below). FSSMF adds a clean numeric cousin: price stretched **>70% above the 30-week EMA is itself a sell pointer** (parabolic/"Eiffel Tower"). 
4. **Relative strength vs benchmark.** A required entry leg in ESRLE/CSLRC, half of FSSDF's "very very powerful signal" (RSI 50 + RS turning green), one of FSSMF's three sell legs, and MSRTM's entire sector layer ("two out of three big market winners were a part of big group advances"). **HARD math, SOFT data** — the ladder fetches no benchmark series.
5. **G2 has real, gate-shaped content on offer.** ESRLE names a forensic checklist: auditor resignation in the last year, contingent liability <15%, promoter pledge. FSSMF supplies a compound sell trigger computable from data already fetched: **debt expanding while operating cash flow stays negative** (Gensol), plus governance-exit clusters (CFO/auditor/board resignations — also WBSNW). This is the first concrete fill for the empty forensic veto; auditor resignation and pledge are screener-fetchable **HARD**, the rest **SOFT**.
6. **Volume — zero footprint in the ladder.** SBFTS's entire subject (breakout valid only on high volume), VPVUV's OBV layer, WESNW/HGBYH's "weight of the evidence." **SOFT** (data not fetched).
7. **Regime conditionality.** "Today sideways market, so can slim often fails" (DSEFD) directly conditions G0; DSEFD also makes entry-pattern choice regime-dependent (breakouts bull / reversals bear / pullbacks sideways). Independently, MAEIM says the EMA/VStop system "will only work in an upwards market... It won't work in a sideways market." L4 therefore supports regime-dependence from two lectures — though as a **JUDGE** call: no lecture gives a numeric regime test (MSRTM's breadth dashboards are the closest computable proxy).
8. **RSI upper caution zone.** TVPDT: crossing "80 to 85... stock should not be purchased now," restated loosely as "75 or 80"; HGBYH: weekly RSI 83-85 = stop **adding** to an existing position. Crucially, four lectures forbid reading high RSI as a sell (FSSMF: "for buying selling this is a worst indicator"; FSSDF's Apar rode RSI>70 from 892 to 6300; TVPD2: "High RSI does not indicate an exit"). So the zone is a fresh-entry/add caution only — **SOFT/JUDGE**, numbers hedged. But note six current CANDIDATEs/WATCHes sit at/inside it (§5).
9. **Scope classifications the ladder can't represent:** core vs satellite portfolio (MAEIM, FSSMF, ESRLE), compounder vs cyclical (multiplier and timeframe choice), investor-style taxonomy (WESNW calls one-rule-for-all "a category error"). **JUDGE.**
10. **Sell-side hygiene rules with clean numbers:** time stop-loss of 4-8 quarters (FSSMF, corroborated WBSNW), trim any position over 12-15% of capital (FSSMF). Portfolio-level, so outside the per-company ladder's current shape, but genuinely rule-like. Pyramiding via Parabolic SAR (WBPNW, CSLRC, ESRLE) — a whole missing concept ("add to a winner"), **HARD**-ish once VStop exists.

## 3. Contradictions — do not smooth these

- **RSI 45 vs 50.** The ladder gates at ≥50 (HOWB). ESRLE states 45 four times ("I will leave it at 45"); CSLRC — documenting the SOIC LTI tool itself — states 45 three times ("in RSI, the bi-signal is always 45"). TVPDT/TVPD2/FSSDF say crossing 50. Same instructor, same tool family, two numbers. The ladder silently picked one source. Names with RSI 45-50 are currently excluded on a threshold the course itself doesn't agree on.
- **RSI required vs optional.** WBPNW's "two basic conditions" are ADX + VStop, RSI demoted to a probability booster; RSCAR's three OR'd entry recipes contain no RSI at all; TVPD2 makes RSI a required third leg. G8 treats it as always-required.
- **ADX: hard gate vs personally disabled.** The ladder hard-codes weekly ADX≥20. MAAIM treats 20/25 as interchangeable and flags 20 as whipsaw-prone; ESRLE calls ADX a toggle ("you can experiment with ADX as 0"); CSLRC's instructor **runs it at zero** ("ADX cases sometimes buy signals late"). The ladder's second technical rule is the one the instructor himself switches off.
- **Timeframe is per-stock, not fixed-weekly.** MAAIM shows the same stock (IIFL) giving entry at 353 on weekly vs 270-290 on monthly, and chooses per business type. G8 hardcodes weekly.
- **Regime.** MAEIM's sideways-market caveat and DSEFD's "can slim often fails" support qualifying G0/G8 by regime; per the coordinator, a Crash Course lecture (SGBT2) did not corroborate the sideways-screen-failure claim. L4's own evidence: two lectures assert it, none contradict it — but the claim is regime commentary dated to the recording, not a tested rule.
- **ATR length 14 vs 10.** HGBYH describes ATR as a 14-period average; TVPDT/MAEIM/FSSMF/TVPD2 set length 10. Any implementation must pick explicitly and record why.
- **Technicals gate vs screen.** DSEFD: "For me, it is never a buy tool." A G8 PASS means "passes his screening filter," never "he would buy here." The ladder's CANDIDATE label flattens this.
- **Fundamentals override technicals, both directions.** HGBYH buys South Indian Bank below RSI 30 on thesis; TVPDT dismisses a red VStop candle on Gujarat Fluorochemicals as promoter debt-paydown selling. The system he teaches is one he consciously overrides — binary gates can't represent that.

**Personal/dated calibrations that must never become single constants:** the VStop multiplier (2 cyclicals / 2.5-3 compounders / 1.5-2 tightened when stretched — explicitly conviction- and volatility-dependent, WBPNW/HGBYH/MAAIM); the RSI ceiling numbers ("75 or 80", "80 to 85", "83-85" — hedged every time); weekly-vs-monthly timeframe; WESNW's 8-12-week base length; SBFTS's base depth <15% and its self-contradicting 3-4 vs 3-5-year breakout ceiling; ESRLE's 200/230 score bars and PE>50 penalty (illustrated by one dated Navin Fluorine episode). On G6: FSSMF's bull/base/bear reverse-DCF is a *process*, not a bar — L4 offers no honest single-constant valuation gate, so leaving G6 empty remains defensible; the ESRLE/FSSMF forensic items mean the same is **no longer true of G2**.

## 4. Named-company evidence: stated vs revealed triggers

Across ~40 worked examples, his revealed practice matches the VStop system, not the RSI/ADX gate: entries fire on ADX/VStop/volume events at named prices (Dixon 1,100, Laurus 183, PI 111, Equitas 56), positions are built in 3-4 tranches on Parabolic SAR, and exits actually execute on VStop flips — Laurus exited at ~520-530 in **three independent lectures** (TVPDT, TVPD2, VPVUV), Dixon at 3,800/4,074, Usha Martin sold from the core PF (ESRLE), South Indian Bank exited on an MD-resignation headline alone (ESRLE). Two further tells: he holds names through years-long consolidations only when a fundamental catalyst justifies the wait (Syngene, MAAIM), and he overrides his own signals when he can explain them (FLUOROCHEM's promoter-sale candle). The revealed system is: fundamental conviction sets scope and multiplier; the trend machine executes; news that breaks the thesis outranks both.
