## 1. CRUX

The lecture is a taxonomy-building exercise: it exists to make the viewer name, out loud, WHICH buying framework (value / momentum / pessimism / special situation / self-help story / speed breaker / bombed-out IPO / techno-funda / technical) they are using every time they buy a stock, on the theory that most retail investors buy without knowing which game they are playing and therefore panic or hold for the wrong reasons later.

## 2. MECHANISM

- Buying approaches differ by causal driver, not by quality: value approach bets on a statistical mean-reversion in a cheap multiple or high yield; momentum bets that earnings flows will keep attracting capital; pessimism bets a one-off event is survivable and reverses; special situation bets on a structural unlock (demerger/reverse merger/self-help capital reallocation); the bombed-out IPO pattern is a compound of fundamentals (post-IPO base normalization) and technicals (lower-highs stopping, 30-week MA turning up).
- Which framework to use is gated by four personal factors, not by the stock: "your approach will depend upon three factors" (WBSNW 00:20:55) — time horizon, expectations, and market environment — plus a fourth he adds afterward, the investor's own temperament/persona.
- The bombed-out IPO mechanism runs on a fixed sequence: pre-IPO margin inflation ("window dressing") (WBSNW 00:32:29) causes the next few post-listing quarters to look like degrowth against an artificially high base, the stock makes lower highs/lower lows for roughly 15-24 months (WBSNW 00:42:15), then bottoms sideways for 9-12 months while being ignored, and only breaks into an uptrend once an earnings surprise confirms the base is real.
- The speed breaker mechanism is a first-principles test applied to a negative news event: judge magnitude (claim size vs. market cap and cash on hand) and structural vs. cyclical nature to decide temporary vs. permanent, then size the position via portfolio allocation rather than avoiding the name outright.
- Special-situation/self-help value creation only compounds if the resulting entity gets SIMPLER (capital moving from a low-ROC segment to a high-ROC one, or debt being refinanced down); a demerger that creates a new complex multi-business entity is explicitly rejected: "If simplification leads to more complexity, you will avoid it." (WBSNW 01:29:20)
- Momentum buying is explicitly NOT price-chasing alone — it requires pairing chart strength (relative strength / 52-week highs, "blue dot list") with confirmed EPS/PAT growth acceleration, else it is just chasing.

If forced to rank: the single most consequential gap is the complete absence of ANY forensic/speed-breaker gate (G2 empty) — this lecture is 100% about how to reason through exactly the kind of one-off negative event a G2 gate would need to arbitrate, and the ladder currently has no rule that could even flag such an event for human review.
