## 1. CRUX

This lecture is not about screening criteria for Indian stocks at all — it is a tool
tour teaching members how to run a first-pass technical/momentum scan over the
INTERNATIONAL (US/European/global) universe using TradingView, IBD, MarketSmith and
a self-built Perplexity "computer use" AI agent, and it is explicit that the scan's
output (a shortlist) is only the entry point into a separate, mandatory fundamental
research step — not a decision by itself.

  stage 2" (SIBES 00:04:22).

## 4. WHAT THE LADDER MISSES

(b) The 30-week EMA filter has NO rule at all in the ladder. The ladder's G8 gate
encodes only entry_rsi-001 (weekly RSI >= 50) and entry_adx-001 (weekly ADX >= 20),
both sourced from HOWB. This lecture independently states the SAME two numbers on
the SAME weekly timeframe, but explicitly groups them with a third, currently-
unencoded filter — price at or above the 30-week EMA — calling all three "momentum
indicators" for the same stage-2 entry condition (SIBES 00:03:48-00:04:22). This is a
genuine gap, not a restatement: a company could satisfy G8 today (RSI+ADX) while
trading below its 30-week EMA, which by this lecture's own logic would not be a
complete "entering stage 2" signal.

(b) The ladder has no rule capturing the "screen is only a first pass" discipline
this lecture treats as its most important point. Every numeric threshold in the
ladder (G0/G1/G3/G8, and the observations) is presented here as something that only
produces a shortlist requiring subsequent, non-computable fundamental research
(reading financials, con calls, filings) before any verdict — "how do I research
them?" (SIBES 00:09:45) is framed as the real work, with screening explicitly
upstream of "manual tools" (SIBES 00:06:29). This isn't a threshold to encode, but it
is a central instructional point with no analog anywhere in the rulebook or
observations: nothing in CONTEXT.md represents "a CANDIDATE verdict is a research
starting point, not a conclusion."

(c) Wolfspeed's "forward with three four times free cash flow" (SIBES 00:25:39) is a
landmine if ever mined for a rule: it is a valuation call on exactly one company, made
in the specific context of a post-bankruptcy, cyclically-inflecting silicon-carbide
power-semiconductor name, not stated as a general FCF-multiple bar for any other
company in this lecture or elsewhere. It should never be encoded as "target forward
P/FCF <= 4x."

Nothing here conditions or narrows the ladder's peg_ratio-001 rule — the number and
direction match exactly, and this lecture's narrower "AI theme" framing is additive
context for one usage, not a restriction the existing F26/F38-sourced scope note is
missing.

A structural point worth flagging even though it isn't a "rule": every number and
company in this lecture concerns the US/European/global market, screened with
US-market tools (TradingView, IBD, MarketSmith) entirely distinct from the
screener.in data source the ladder's 38-name Indian shortlist is built on. None of
this lecture's screening mechanics were demonstrated on, or claimed to apply
verbatim to, Indian equities.
