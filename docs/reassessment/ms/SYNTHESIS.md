## 1. The through-line, and where the show disagrees with the courses

This corpus is not a course. It is a weekly commentary/tooling broadcast (Feb-Sep 2026) whose organizing question is *where is the market's money moving this week* — sector rotation, breadth, order flow, corporate actions — with company-level screening bolted on as a discovery step, never as a verdict. Its own theory of returns puts stock-picking last: "60% of a stock's move is attributable to the theme, 20% is attributable to the liquidity and last 20% is attributable to your stock picking" (MS2806A 00:03:05). Twenty-three of 39 briefs report that a screen output is a reading prompt, not a decision — "screening doesn't find multi-packers, it finds reading worthy candidates." (MS1503A 01:04:37); "You can't just buy a screen or a scan and not buy a stock. You have to develop a framework." (MS2203 02:04:46-02:04:49). Fourteen briefs honestly report that nothing in their lecture conditions any of the 7 rules or 21 observations (MS0305A/B, MS0706A, MS1204A/B, MS1406A, MS1904, MS1907B, MS2106A, MS2604A/B, MS2607B, MS2806B, MS3008A). The ladder, by construction, encodes the 20% and has no slot for the 60%.

Three drifts against the course material the rulebook was built from:

- **Screen → sector → screen.** The Crash synthesis recorded a bottom-up→top-down drift; this corpus is where the top-down half actually lives, as a *tool*: SOIC's working screen is not two independent thresholds but one blended score — "these scores is a combination of ADX, relative strength, RSI, stage analysis and everything is just coded into this score" (MS1005A 00:21:05), re-computed weekly as a sector-level "score change" (MS1705 00:43:04; MS2806B 00:24:05). The ladder's G8 evaluates two of those inputs, independently, per company.
- **Level → inflection.** The course thresholds (G0 15/20) are restated here (MS2607A 00:15:26 is an exact match) but the show's own hunting screens are level-*shift* tests: "today's earnings are more than 30%" against a range-bound trailing base (MS1005B 00:14:52), "companies whose pad broke 30" (MS0803A NO-TS), and a growth-*stage* read — "If any business is into the 12th quarter of accelerating pad growth. So you better bet that's a structural story. Otherwise, reversion to mean is coming because growth rates revert to mean." (MS2203 01:46:43-01:46:50). Same number, different meaning depending on which quarter of the streak it is; G0 cannot tell a fresh inflection from a maturing run.
- **Valuation as band → valuation as sentiment, by sector.** "growth drives valuation and not valuation which drives growth" (MS0507 00:55:28); "Any sector where 30-40-50 percentage top-line growth is there, you should study it by leaving your P-Ratio thought process behind" (MS2607A 00:54:39); "trailing 12 month P ratio kills the J curve mindset while it is like taking birth." (MS1207A 00:23:10); "Reverse DCF is far better" (MS2806A 00:07:22). Six weeks later the same instructor caps an entire sector's multiple: "70-80 PE companies will not get it in cables and wires. I think 30-40, maybe 45-50 times might be the peak PE ratios for these companies." (MS0609 00:13:46). `pe_context-001`'s flat 15-35 band is neither voice.

Honest summary of what transfers: on the numbers the ladder already gates (15/20, 0.7, 15, 50/20) this corpus is corroboration-or-silence, not correction — no MS lecture states a D/E figure, none uses RSI 45, and the G0/market-cap-1000 trio is restated verbatim once (MS2607A 00:15:26). The corpus's real content is the *signal categories* the rulebook has no field for (§3).
