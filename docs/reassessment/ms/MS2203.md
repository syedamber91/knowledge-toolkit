## 1. CRUX

This lecture is not a quant screen at all -- it is a demonstration of the discretionary, story-driven curation step that sits BEFORE and AFTER the ladder's numeric gates: how to pull raw names out of ready-made StockScans screens (index-outperformance, margin-expansion, PAT-growth) and manually decide which of them deserve a watchlist slot, using promoter behaviour, capacity-expansion stories, sector rotation, and a "young vs. old momentum" business-cycle read that no ratio alone can supply.

## 2. MECHANISM

- Start from macro/sector context (here: the Strait of Hormuz oil shock, dollar/bond-yield stress, GLP-1 drug disruption, IT-sector pessimism) to decide which sectors deserve attention right now -- "Signs of sectoral rotation are visible again... One is Pharma[ceuticals]. Second one is Power Sector." (MS2203 00:41:37-00:41:43).
- Pull candidate names from pre-built StockScans screens -- index/relative outperformance over the trailing 3 months, near-all-time-highs, margin expansion, explosive PAT growth -- as the raw discovery funnel: "So that's the fundamental trigger, that will screen your process and put it into the watchlist." (MS2203 01:23:01).
- For each candidate, test for "uniqueness" (structural necessity, non-commodity, market-share leadership, avoiding margin-dilutive segments) using DRHPs, concalls, promoter-interview series, and an AI prompt template rather than a ratio: "structural uniqueness or non cyclical non commodity element." (MS2203 00:59:13) and "leadership can see display when the revenue growth is far higher than the industry and They keep adding new clients and they hold number one or number two positions in the industry and they avoid margin dilutive segments." (MS2203 00:59:25-00:59:35).
- Check promoter conviction directly: "one thing is very important that what is the promoter doing? Is the promoter selling or buying?" (MS2203 01:00:55-01:00:58), operationalised via corporate actions such as a warrants issue -- "When the promoter himself is infusing money into the business" (MS2203 01:01:23).
- Classify the growth story's stage in a "young momentum vs. old (mature) momentum" fundamental-growth lifecycle, not just its latest YoY print, because a rich valuation on an already-mature growth streak is a trap even when relative strength looks fine: "If any business is into the 12th quarter of accelerating pad growth. So you better bet that's a structural story. Otherwise, reversion to mean is coming because growth rates revert to mean." (MS2203 01:46:43-01:46:50).
- Cross-check peers inside the same scan hit before committing research time -- comparing PAT-growth trajectories across sector peers and screening out a peer with a known red flag: "I will go to the same industry and see all the peers... Keynes had some accounting issues in the last 3-4 months." (MS2203 01:33:10-01:33:24).
- Treat the screen/scan as discovery only, never as a buy decision on its own, and fold the survivors into a separate four-part buy framework (Theme, Valuation, Growth, Promoter): "You can't just buy a screen or a scan and not buy a stock. You have to develop a framework." (MS2203 02:04:46-02:04:49); "So, theme valuation, growth and promoter. I use a framework by doing TVGP." (MS2203 02:11:31-02:11:33).

## 4. WHAT THE LADDER MISSES

(b) Growth-momentum STAGE has no rule or observation at all, and it directly qualifies the two growth gates that do exist. canslim_sales-001 (sales_growth_yoy_pct >= 15) and canslim_pat-001 (pat_growth_yoy_pct >= 20) both test one quarter's YoY print in isolation. This lecture states outright that the SAME number means something different depending on which quarter of the streak it is: quarter 1-3 of acceleration ("young momentum") is the priority buy zone, while a company already many quarters into an accelerating run ("If any business is into the 12th quarter of accelerating pad growth... Otherwise, reversion to mean is coming" MS2203 01:46:43-01:46:50) is a name to be cautious of, not to newly add. The ladder's G0 has no way to distinguish a fresh inflection from a maturing streak about to decelerate -- this is a genuinely new, unencoded dimension, not a restatement of growth_durability-001 (which only reads the size of 3-year growth, not its stage/duration).
