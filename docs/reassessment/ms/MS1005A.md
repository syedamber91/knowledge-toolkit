## 1. CRUX

This is not a company-screening lesson — it is the periodic (every 14-20 days) top-down "where is the market's money moving right now" briefing: which sub-sectors and market-cap segments currently show rising relative-strength/rotation scores and reaccelerating earnings, so a member reallocates attention toward the CURRENT sector cycle instead of chasing whichever sector worked in a prior cycle.

## 2. MECHANISM

- Runs a proprietary "stock scans" sectoral rotation tool that ranks sub-sectors and broad indices by a composite score, explicitly not a single indicator: "these scores is a combination of ADX, relative strength, RSI, stage analysis and everything is just coded into this score" (MS1005A 00:21:05).
- Frames sector leadership as long, multi-year regimes rather than a fixed checklist: 2010-2020 favored branded consumer names (Page Industries, Asian Paints, Astral, Berger, Bata, Relaxo named as the era's winners), while "this seems to be the decade of these capital heavy businesses or asset intensive businesses" (MS1005A 00:06:12) — capital markets, manufacturing/electrification, defense/aerospace, CDMO, niche exporters — "But today is the cycle of manufacturing Asset heavy businesses Capital market players Maybe some of the niche exporters but cycle is not for consumers." (MS1005A 00:08:43)
- Never trusts a technical/rotation signal alone — requires a fundamental catalyst before acting on it: for Nifty Pharma's base breakout he explicitly warns "past is not the right indicator." (MS1005A 00:13:20) and "You have to combine it with the results." (MS1005A 00:13:28) The catalyst offered is a capital-migration logic: "In Nifty IT, companies are growing at 2-3 percentage types." (MS1005A 00:12:27) versus Pharma's mid-teens-to-twenties growth, so "there's a possibility of money shifting from Nifty IT to Nifty Pharma is that a possibility that exists because Nifty IT is showing no growth at all." (MS1005A 00:12:54)
- Reads earnings-season BREADTH across market-cap tiers, not just individual stocks, to size how broad the current growth reacceleration is: "500 companies have declared the results which is median pad growth is 25% operating profit growth is 23% and median revenue growth is 15%" (MS1005A 00:21:34), then compares Nifty 50 (~10% PAT growth) against Nifty Midcap150 (~25.6%) and Smallcap250/Microcap250 (~25-30%) in the same continuous passage (MS1005A 00:23:31) to argue the growth is concentrated away from the large-cap index.
- Links global capex/earnings trends to Indian proxies as a leading indicator: US hyperscaler cloud growth and the "five layers of AI" (chips, infrastructure, models, applications, energy) framework are used to justify why Indian cable, transformer, aerospace-parts and power-semi names are showing strength.
- Tracks corporate-action data from the same "stock scans" tool as an independent alpha source — specifically promoter warrant issuance and capacity-expansion announcements: "corporate announcements sometimes have a lot of alpha" (MS1005A 01:05:00), with warrants singled out as a strong signal because "Do promoters lock in the price at a warrant price?" - Explicitly separates a rotation/earnings thesis from a valuation call — sector strength is a reason to look, not a reason to buy at any price. He repeats this discipline explicitly about the capital-market theme: "But I do not want to spend a lot of businesses. We will be market losses. We will remove them." (MS1005A 00:08:14)
