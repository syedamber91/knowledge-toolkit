## 1. CRUX

geopolitically-triggered smallcap/midcap drawdown by showing that market-breadth and
index-valuation data say this is a late-stage contraction rather than a fresh bear leg, so the
decision to change is behavioral (hold discipline, size fresh equity allocation off these
readings, avoid narrative-crowded richly-valued names) — and, in the second half, to model his
own idea-sourcing process (reading every con-call in a sector, sector by sector, with Claude as
an assistant) so viewers copy the *process*, not the specific names.

  quantitative gauges of how much bad news is priced in: "this number has again fallen to 26%
  right" (MS0803B 00:23:56) against a bull-market norm where the same reading is "hovering above
  85 to 90%" (MS0803B 00:24:28), and on valuation, "if the market falls like further 5 to 7
  percentage from there then we will start going into P of 20s" (MS0803B 00:23:07).

  temperamental and portfolio-level, not a stock-specific tactic: accept the drawdown, avoid
  chasing narrative-crowded richly valued names, and use asset-allocation levers (INVITs, gold
  ETFs, debt, or simply raising equity/mutual-fund allocation) rather than trying to time an exact
  bottom.
- Separately, he sources new ideas not from a ratio screen but by reading every con-call in one
  sector at a time, looking for capacity expansion, new customer wins, and margin inflections that
  reveal "this is a new sector that is getting created" (MS0803B 00:34:07) before it would show up

- He treats Claude as a research assistant for exactly that workflow — building a con-call-based
