## 1. CRUX

This is a weekly market-commentary show doing two jobs at once: (a) tell the viewer where the broader Indian market's health/regime stands right now and which sectors are rotating into strength, and (b) walk one niche sector (power/energy piping) end to end so the viewer can screen it using qualitative moat criteria (certifications, approvals, backward integration) that no ratio in the ladder captures.
