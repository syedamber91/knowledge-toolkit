## 1. CRUX

This is a weekly market-commentary broadcast whose actual job is to break the
audience's habit of reading "the market" through the Nifty 50 headline index
at all -- the instructor's real argument is that the current bull run is
narrow, manufacturing-led, and running almost entirely OUTSIDE the large-cap
index (foreign institutional selling is concentrated in the old-economy
large-cap names), so a screener anchored to index membership or index-style
"quality" will structurally miss where the money and the earnings growth
actually are right now: "we should get out of the mindset of index"
(MS2106B 00:41:48), and, one sentence later, "Real bull market is going out
of the index" (MS2106B 00:41:53).
